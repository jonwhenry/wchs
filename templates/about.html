About the Society

The Waller County Historical Society is dedicated to preserving, protecting, and promoting the rich history of Waller County, Texas. Founded by local historians and passionate citizens, our mission is to ensure that the stories, artifacts, and heritage of our community are passed down to future generations.

We work tirelessly to maintain local archives, support historical markers, and operate the Waller County Historical Museum. Through educational programs, exhibits, and community outreach, we bring the past to life for residents and visitors alike.
